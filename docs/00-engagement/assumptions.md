# Initial Assumptions

These assumptions must be validated during discovery.

- The client uses email as a major communication channel.
- Administrative workflows currently require significant manual effort.
- The client uses a digital calendar.
- The client may use CRM and accounting software.
- AI can assist with understanding unstructured requests.
- Some business actions can be safely automated.
- High-risk actions will require human approval.
- The initial POC will use simulated or test business data.
- AWS will be the primary cloud platform.
